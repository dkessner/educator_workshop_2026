View from the Frontier:  AI Use in Industry and Academia

In the four years since the launch of ChatGPT, AI has progressed from a
curiosity to an essential tool for research in academia and industry.
Researchers worldwide are using AI tools for analyzing and visualizing data,
writing software, and exploring new research areas.  In this presentation I
will give a tour of some of the ways that AI is currently being used in
research and software development, based on my work at the Ellison Medical
Institute.  How does this affect STEM education?  What should we emphasize in
Math and Science courses to prepare students?  What should we be teaching in
Computer Science classes?  I don't have all the answers, but I'll share my
thoughts.


Darren Kessner is the Program Head in Computer Science and Software
Innovation at Marlborough School in Los Angeles.  Darren is also a Senior
Software Engineer on the AI and Advanced Molecular Medicine team at the Ellison
Medical Institute.  Darren is a strong proponent of free and open source
software, open public data, and open educational resources. 


Target audience:
Math, Science, and Computer Science teachers (High School)
Administrators
